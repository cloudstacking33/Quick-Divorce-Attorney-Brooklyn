What Is an Uncontested Divorce?

An uncontested divorce is one in which both spouses agree to end the marriage and agree on the terms, or one spouse does not oppose the case. The agreement needs to cover every issue that applies to your family. These may include division of property and debts, spousal maintenance, child custody, parenting time, child support, and health insurance.

An uncontested case still goes through the New York Supreme Court. Papers must be prepared, filed, served, and reviewed before a judgment can be granted. Many uncontested matters are decided on written submissions, so spouses often do not need a trial. A Simple Divorce Lawyer can help you identify which forms and sworn statements your situation calls for, and can point out gaps before the court does.

Simple does not mean careless. A home, a retirement account, a family business, or children under 21 all deserve close attention. Terms that seem clear in a conversation may be unclear on paper, and unclear terms can cause disputes years later.

No Fault Divorce and Residency in New York

New York allows a no fault divorce when the relationship has broken down irretrievably for at least six months and one spouse states this under oath. You do not have to prove that your spouse did something wrong. The financial and child related issues still need to be resolved by agreement or decided by the court before the divorce is final.

The state also has residency rules. In many cases, you or your spouse must have lived in New York continuously for two years before filing. A one year period, or another path, may apply when there are certain connections to the state, such as where you married, where you lived as a couple, or where the grounds arose. Please confirm your eligibility before any papers are filed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do both spouses need to go to court for an uncontested divorce?

Often no. Many uncontested divorces in New York are reviewed on written papers. The court can still ask for more information or schedule an appearance.

How long does an uncontested divorce take in Brooklyn?

There is no guaranteed timeline. It depends on how soon documents are completed, whether the papers are accurate, how your spouse is served or responds, and the court workload.

What is a no fault divorce in New York?

It is a divorce based on the relationship having broken down irretrievably for at least six months, stated under oath. You do not need to prove misconduct.

What if my spouse will not sign the papers?

A refusal to sign does not automatically end the case. The next step depends on whether your spouse was properly served, responds, or disputes the terms. Ask an attorney to review the facts.

Can I file if I cannot find my spouse?

It may be possible. You generally need to make serious efforts to locate your spouse and then ask the court for permission to use another method of service.

Can one lawyer represent both spouses?

One lawyer should not advocate for both sides in a divorce. Each spouse should have a chance to get independent legal advice.
